# Generic scoring playbook (detailed)

## Rank high

- Laughter, cheering, sustained applause
- Surprise reveals ("today we're announcing", "the winner is")
- Debate climax, mic-drop quotes, standing ovation cues in text
- Speaker emphasis: repetition, rhetorical questions answered immediately

## Audio cues

- Sustained energy_peak above video median
- Sudden step without corresponding words → investigate neighbor windows before scoring high

## Skip (score ≤3)

- "Um", filler, long pauses with empty text
- Repeated sponsor or housekeeping segments
- Near-identical wording in adjacent windows (keep one)

## Editing rules

- Favor sentence boundaries; avoid cutting mid-word in transcript
- Spread picks across timeline (early, middle, late thirds)
- Target total duration via fewer high-score picks rather than many medium picks

## Keyword hints

amazing, incredible, wow, breakthrough, finally, winner, applause, laughter, thank you, introduce
