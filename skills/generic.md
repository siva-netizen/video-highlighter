# What is a highlight (rank high)
- Emotional peaks in speech (laughter, surprise, applause)
- Clear narrative beats (announcement, punchline, reveal)
- High audio energy with meaningful words (not noise alone)

# Audio cues
- Laughter, cheering, sustained applause
- Speaker emphasis and faster pace

# Skip
- Long silences, filler, sponsor reads
- Duplicate segments with near-identical wording

# Editing rules
- Favor complete thoughts (full sentence boundaries)
- Spread picks across the timeline
