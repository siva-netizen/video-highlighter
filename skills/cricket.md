# What is a highlight (rank high)
- Wickets (bowled, caught, lbw, run out, stumped)
- Boundaries and sixes with excited commentary
- Milestones (50, 100) and match-turning spells
- Sharp crowd roar + commentator pitch rise

# Audio cues
- Sudden loudness spike with "gone", "massive", "what a shot"
- Sustained crowd noise after a boundary

# Skip
- Drinks breaks, DRS replays without outcome, ads, static wide shots with no action
- Routine singles and dot balls unless part of a wicket sequence

# Editing rules
- Prefer full delivery-to-reaction arc for wickets
- Do not double-count same ball (live + slow-mo replay)
