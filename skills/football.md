# What is a highlight (rank high)
- Goals and penalties scored
- World-class saves and woodwork hits
- Red cards and major VAR decisions with clear outcome
- Fast breaks ending in chance or goal

# Audio cues
- Commentator volume jump, "GOAL!", crowd sustained cheer

# Skip
- Throw-ins, goal kicks, offside without shot
- Repeated replay-only segments if live angle already selected

# Editing rules
- Include build-up for goals when commentary references it
- Keep post-goal celebration through first crowd peak
